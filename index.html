DAY 5 / 10

Things I Notice
About You 💖

You probably don't even realize how many tiny things about you stay in my mind. But I notice them. Always.

🌙 The Way You Text When Sleepy
Your messages become softer. More random. Sometimes half words. Sometimes unnecessary honesty. And somehow that becomes the cutest version of you. It's like your heart starts texting before your brain does.
💭 When You Overthink
You try acting normal first. But your energy changes slightly. Your replies become slower. Your words become smaller. And I immediately know something is wrong. I don't know how. I just feel it.
💕 Your Possessiveness
You act annoyed first. Loving later. And honestly? It's adorable. Because underneath all that attitude is someone who just loves deeply and fears losing what matters to her.
🎧 Your Voice
It's actually unfair. Because somehow one “hello” from you changes my entire mood. One call from you can fix a day that was going horribly. I don't think you realize how much comfort your voice carries.
🫀 The Way You Care
You don't even realize how naturally you make people feel safe. The way you ask things. The way you remember tiny details. The way your softness appears when someone needs it. You carry warmth so naturally.
✨ Things You Don't Know You Do To Me
You make me reread chats. You make songs hit differently. You make random places remind me of you. You make me smile at my screen like an idiot. And somehow... you make the future feel less scary.
You know what I think is the most beautiful thing about you? Not just your smile. Not your looks. Not even the way you love.

It's the fact that your presence changes people emotionally. Especially me.

Before you, days were just days. Now tiny things matter more. Songs feel deeper. Late nights feel softer. Birthdays feel exciting. Future feels warmer.

And maybe that's why I pay attention to these little details about you. Because loving someone deeply makes even the smallest things feel important. The way you type. The way you get jealous. The way you become quiet. The way you suddenly become extra soft. The way you care without even realizing it.

I notice all of it, baby. All of it. And I think I always will.

Because somewhere between all these tiny moments... you quietly became my favorite person. ❤️
for GK 🤍 “love is sometimes just noticing little things forever.”
