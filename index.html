DAY 5 / 10

Things I Notice
About You 💖

You probably don't even realize how deeply I observe you. Not because I try to. But because when someone becomes important to your heart... even their smallest habits start feeling special. And somehow baby... every little thing about you stayed with me.

👓 The Way You Adjust Your Specs
You probably think it's nothing. Just a random habit. But I notice it every single time. The way your fingers softly push your specs back up while you're focused or talking seriously. God... I don't know why that tiny thing affects me emotionally so much. Maybe because it's so naturally you. So effortless. So unintentionally beautiful. And in those moments I just stare at you and think: “How did someone this adorable become my person?”
🌙 The Way You Text When Sleepy
Your messages become softer. More random. More honest somehow. Sometimes incomplete. Sometimes emotional in the cutest way possible. It's like your heart starts speaking before your brain does. And honestly? Sleepy you feels so precious to me. Like someone I want to protect from every harsh thing in this world.
🤍 The Way You Deliberately Rub Yourself On Me
You think I don't notice it. But I do. Every single time. The way you casually come closer. The way your shoulder stays touching mine longer than needed. The way you purposely brush against me and then act innocent after doing it. Baby... those tiny touches stay in my chest for hours. Because your closeness never feels normal to me. It feels calming. Safe. Like for a few seconds the whole world becomes quieter.
😂 The Way You Find Double Meanings In Everything
I swear your brain works differently. I say one completely normal sentence and suddenly you are staring at me with THAT look. Then comes your smirk. Then your laugh. Then somehow I end up blushing because YOU made it dirty. Honestly? Your mind is dangerous. But it's also one of the reasons talking to you never feels boring. You somehow turn ordinary moments into memories.
💕 The Way You Laugh
Your laugh does something to me emotionally. Especially when it's genuine. That uncontrollable laugh where you forget the whole world for a second. I think those are the moments where you look the most beautiful. Not because of your face. But because happiness fits your soul perfectly. And hearing you laugh somehow makes my own heart feel lighter too.
💭 When You Overthink
You try acting normal first. But your energy changes slightly. Your replies become slower. Your words become smaller. And I immediately know something is wrong. I don't know how. I just feel it. And during those moments all I want is to hold you close enough for your mind to finally feel quiet and safe again. I wish you could see yourself through my eyes sometimes. Maybe then you would understand how lovable you truly are.
🫀 The Way You Care
You care in tiny ways. And somehow those tiny ways destroy me emotionally the most. The way you remember details. The way your voice changes when you worry. The way you ask if I ate. The way your softness appears instantly when I am not okay. The way you quietly stay when someone needs you emotionally. You don't even realize how healing your presence feels sometimes. You make love feel gentle instead of scary.
✨ Things You Don't Know You Do To Me
You make me reread chats. You make songs feel deeper. You make random places remind me of you. You make me smile at my screen like an idiot. You make my mood change because of one notification. You make me imagine future moments randomly. And somehow... you made life feel softer. Safer. Warmer. Like maybe the world won't feel so heavy as long as you are somewhere beside me.
You know what the craziest thing is? None of these are “big” moments. They are tiny things. Tiny habits. Tiny expressions. Tiny little versions of you.

But that's exactly how love became dangerous for me. Not suddenly. Not loudly. Not through one dramatic moment.

It happened slowly. Through your laugh. Through your sleepy texts. Through the way you adjust your specs. Through your random jealousy. Through the way your voice softens when you care. Through your tiny touches. Through your stupid double meanings. Through the way your eyes look when you genuinely feel something.

And one day without realizing it... you became emotionally attached to every peaceful feeling inside me.

Now whenever something good happens, I want to tell you first. Whenever life hurts, my heart searches for you first. Whenever I imagine comfort, I somehow imagine you beside me.

And maybe that's why I notice these little things so deeply. Because when someone truly becomes important to your soul... nothing about them stays small anymore.

Everything matters. Every smile. Every habit. Every touch. Every “goodnight.” Every little version of you.

And honestly baby... I think I could spend an entire lifetime noticing tiny things about you and still never get tired of loving you. ❤️
