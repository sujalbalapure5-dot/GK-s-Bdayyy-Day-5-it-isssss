DAY 5 / 10

Things I Notice
About You 💖

You probably don't even realize how many tiny things about you stay inside my head permanently. But I notice them. Every single one of them. And somehow those little things became some of my favorite parts of life.

👓 The Way You Adjust Your Specs
You probably think it's just a random habit. But I notice it every single time. The way your fingers softly push your specs back up while you're focused or talking seriously. God... I don't know why that tiny thing affects me so much emotionally. Maybe because it's so naturally you. So effortless. So unintentionally beautiful. Those tiny moments make me stop and realize: “Yeah... this girl really has my whole heart.”
🌙 The Way You Text When Sleepy
Your messages become softer. More random. Sometimes incomplete. Sometimes emotionally honest in the cutest way possible. It's like your heart starts speaking before your brain does. And honestly? Sleepy you feels so precious to me. Like someone I want to protect forever.
🤍 The Way You Come Closer To Me
You think I don't notice it. But I do. The way you deliberately brush against me. The way your shoulder stays touching mine a little longer than needed. The way you act innocent after doing it. Baby... those tiny touches stay with me for hours. Because your closeness never feels ordinary to me. It feels like comfort. Like home somehow found physical form.
😂 The Way You Find Double Meanings
I swear your brain works differently. I say one normal sentence and suddenly you are staring at me with that look. Then comes your smirk. Then your laugh. And somehow I end up blushing because YOU made it dirty. Honestly? Your mind is dangerous. And unbelievably adorable. I love how easily you bring playful happiness into moments.
💕 The Way You Laugh
Your laugh does something to me emotionally. Especially when it's genuine. That uncontrollable laugh where you completely forget the world for a second. I think in those moments you look the most beautiful. Not because of your face. But because happiness fits your soul perfectly. And hearing you laugh somehow makes my chest feel lighter too.
💭 When You Overthink
You try acting normal first. But your energy changes slightly. Your replies become slower. Your words become smaller. And I immediately know something is wrong. I don't know how. I just feel it. And during those moments all I want is to hold you close enough for your mind to finally feel quiet and safe again.
🫀 The Way You Care
You care in tiny ways. And somehow those tiny ways destroy me emotionally the most. The way you remember details. The way your voice changes when you worry. The way you ask if I ate. The way your softness appears instantly when I am not okay. You don't even realize how healing your presence feels sometimes. You make love feel gentle instead of scary.
✨ Things You Don't Know You Do To Me
You make me reread chats. You make songs feel deeper. You make random places remind me of you. You make my mood change from one notification. You make me smile at my phone like an idiot. And somehow... you make the future feel softer. Safer. Warmer. Like maybe life won't be that hard as long as you are somewhere beside me.
You know what the craziest thing is? None of these are “big” moments. They are tiny things. Tiny habits. Tiny expressions. Tiny details.

But that's exactly how love became dangerous for me. Not through one dramatic moment. Not suddenly. Not loudly.

It happened slowly. Through your laugh. Through your sleepy texts. Through your random care. Through your voice. Through the way your eyes soften when you genuinely feel something.

And one day without realizing it... you became emotionally attached to every peaceful feeling inside me.

Now whenever something good happens, I want to tell you first. Whenever life hurts, my heart searches for you first. Whenever I imagine comfort, I somehow imagine you beside me.

And maybe that's why I notice these little things so deeply. Because when someone truly becomes important to your soul... nothing about them stays “small” anymore.

Everything matters. Every smile. Every habit. Every touch. Every “goodnight.” Every version of you.

And honestly baby... I think I could spend an entire lifetime noticing tiny things about you and still never get tired of loving you. ❤️
for GK 🤍 “love is sometimes just noticing little things forever.”
